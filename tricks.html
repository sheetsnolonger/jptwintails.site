TRICKS

a simple glossary of common skateboarding tricks and terminology.

basics flip tricks shove its grinds stance
ollie jumping with the board without grabbing it.
manual balancing on either the front or back wheels.
kickflip an ollie where the board flips beneath you.
heelflip a flip in the opposite direction of a kickflip.
pop shove-it the board rotates 180 degrees underneath you.
360 shove-it the board rotates a full 360 degrees.
50-50 both trucks grind along an obstacle.
boardslide the middle of the deck slides across an obstacle.
nosegrind grinding primarily on the front truck.
regular left foot forward while riding.
goofy right foot forward while riding.
frontside describes the direction of a rotation.
