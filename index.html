03

the basics

TRICKS

learn the language before you learn the trick.

olliejump with the board without grabbing it.
kickflipan ollie with a flip along the board's long axis.
heelflipa flip in the opposite direction from a kickflip.
pop shove-itthe board rotates 180° underneath you.
boardslideslide with the middle of the deck across an obstacle.
50-50both trucks lock onto a ledge or rail.
frontside / backsidenames the direction your body rotates around an obstacle.
regular / goofyyour natural riding stance: left foot or right foot forward.
